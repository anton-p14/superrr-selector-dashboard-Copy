Methodology

1 · Pressure definition

Second innings only; static Cricsheet target; 20 overs; no D/L, Super Over or known target ambiguity. State is computed before each delivery. Pressure requires positive runs remaining, 1–30 legal balls remaining, and required run rate at or above the competition-specific 75th percentile.

Frozen thresholds: IPL 12.1895 runs/over · T20I 11.8333 runs/over.

2 · Performance metrics

Batters: batter runs; balls faced exclude wides and include no-balls; strike rate = 100 × runs / balls; improvement = pressure SR − usual SR.

Bowlers: legal balls exclude wides/no-balls; candidate bowler-charged runs deduct byes, leg-byes and penalty extras; candidate credited wickets are bowled, caught, caught and bowled, lbw, stumped and hit wicket. Economy = 6 × charged runs / legal balls; wicket rate = 100 × credited wickets / legal balls.

3 · Eligibility and ranking

At least 12 pressure batting balls or bowling legal balls, at least 30 non-pressure baseline opportunities, and pressure appearances in at least two matches. Within-competition percentile = 100 × (N − rank) / (N − 1). Combined score is the mean of available IPL/T20I percentiles; players are merged across competitions only by exact Cricsheet registry ID.

Candidate pools: 65 IPL / 55 T20I batters; 91 IPL / 105 T20I bowlers. Bowler composite uses 60% standardised economy improvement and 40% standardised wicket-rate improvement.

4 · Validation and limitations

79,326 selected delivery states were replayed against source JSON and reconciled to player-level aggregates. However, full-population official scorecard validation and T20I scorecard validation were not available. Three IPL event-count mismatches remain unresolved; 91 local T20I records remain unmatched. Candidate wicket-credit and scoring rules are not fully season-validated.

Top-10 overlap at 6 / 18 / 24 pressure opportunities: IPL batters 9/8/8; T20I batters 10/8/6; IPL bowlers 9/6/3; T20I bowlers 7/3/3. Bowler ordering is especially sensitive to sample cutoff.
